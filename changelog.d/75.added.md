`phylo.qa.ledger`: a ledger of benchmarked and validated runs, recorded with
Aim. `RunManifest` makes `infra/CLAUDE.md`'s replay rule executable — commit,
both lockfile hashes, seed, dataset, model, move set, evaluation budget and
hardware are all required, and an incomplete manifest raises rather than
recording an unreplayable run. Aim is optional, in a new `tracking` extra.
