`snakes_and_ladders.opt.fit.standard_errors_at` gives a fit an interval from the observed information at that point, whatever optimizer produced it. Every objective now implements `theta_from`, the inverse of its constraint map, so an expectation-maximization fit — which never builds an unconstrained vector — can be given the same interval a gradient fit gets. The refusals are unchanged: a point where the information is singular, indefinite or ill-conditioned is still refused rather than given a meaningless interval.
