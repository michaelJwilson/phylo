Added `phylo.sim.graph.PottsGraph` and `lattice_graph`, an N-D lattice constructor over open or periodic boundaries, and `phylo.sim.potts.simulate_potts`, a `k`-state Potts sampler on any such graph: exact backward-message sampling on an open 1-D chain, single-site Gibbs/heat-bath MCMC otherwise. `phylo.opt.potts.simulate_chains` now builds its chain as the `N = 1` case of the same construction instead of carrying its own copy of the sampling recursion.
