`docs/` gains its own `CLAUDE.md`, on the pattern of the other module files.
It carries the formatting contract that follows from the document's expected
reader — which root `CLAUDE.md` stated and now points to — together with the
rules that keep a committed, CI-regenerated artifact true: what is committed
against what is generated, that a figure is regenerated rather than edited,
and that a generated caption may report only quantities continuous in their
inputs, since CI rebuilds `docs/draft.pdf` and byte-compares it.
