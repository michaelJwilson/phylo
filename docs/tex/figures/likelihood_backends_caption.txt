Backend agreement. (a) All three backends against brute-force marginalization over internal states on a 4-taxon tree at 400 sites, where the exhaustive sum is tractable and is an independent algorithm rather than a second opinion from the same recursion; largest deviation 2.05e-12, within the 1e-09 absolute tolerance the regression tests pin at these sizes. (b) The accelerated backends against the vectorized NumPy reference at full fixture sizes, where brute force is not available, plotted as relative deviation; largest 7.76e-13, at the float64 floor (dotted). Relative rather than absolute because the total log-likelihood is a sum over sites: the same agreement reads as 7.39e-07 absolute at these sizes, so an absolute tolerance fixed at small sizes does not transfer. Points at the axis floor are exact agreement.
